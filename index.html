About

I am an Assistant Professor of Electrical Engineering at King Saud University. My work combines power systems with operations research. My main focus today is the power supply of electrified railways and metro systems.

Before joining KSU, I was an Academic Visitor at the University of Oxford and a Research Associate at the University of Manchester, where I worked on the EU Horizon 2020 project JUMP2Excel. I received my PhD in Electrical Engineering with a minor in Industrial Engineering from Iowa State University, my MSE from Arizona State University, and my BSc with first class honours from King Saud University.
